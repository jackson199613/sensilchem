Where we meet the industry

In short: We exhibit where the industries we serve meet — coatings, plastics, feed additives and powder processing. Small volumes, multiple grades and hands-on application talk are easier face to face, so these are the shows we keep coming back to.

Scenes from the exhibitions we attend

K Show

Düsseldorf, Germany

Plastics and rubber. Where masterbatch dispersion and film antiblock get tested against real extrusion lines.

Pacific Coatings Show

Jakarta, Indonesia

Coatings in South-East Asia. Matting agents judged on humidity, resin systems and local production conditions.

American Coatings Show

Indianapolis, USA

Coatings in North America. Gloss targets, rheology and regulatory pressure discussed directly with formulators.

European Coatings Show

Nuremberg, Germany

The coatings industry's main meeting point. Raw materials, lab equipment and formulation trends in one place.

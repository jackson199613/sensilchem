CENSIL grades

Eighteen grades in six families. Each entry explains what the grade is for and which process problem it addresses. We do not publish specification tables — technical data sheets, specifications and COA are issued per enquiry, at the current supplier revision, with a suggested test plan.

Supplied in three physical forms

Which form suits you is usually decided by how the material is fed, dosed and handled on your line — not by the chemistry alone.

Powder

The default form across most grades — carriers, porous grades, matting silica, hydrophobic silica and antiblock powders.

Micropearl

Free-flowing rounded beads. Lower dust and easier dosing where handling and metering matter more than fineness.

Granular

Coarse free-flowing particles used where feeding stability and dust control drive the choice — the GR grades for masterbatch.

Carriers & flow aids

Built to take a liquid active onto a dry particle and keep the resulting powder workable. The three grades sit on one absorption gradient, so a trial can move up or down the ladder without changing platform.

CENSIL 260P

Precipitated silica powder

The base carrier. Start here when the liquid load is moderate and the real problem is flow and caking rather than raw absorption capacity. Widely used in vitamin and trace-element premixes and as an anti-caking aid in dry food blends.

Used on: Feed Additive Flow & Carrier · Food Powder Flow · Agrochemical Carrier

CENSIL 265P

Precipitated silica powder

The middle route. Takes more liquid than the base grade while keeping the powder free-running — the usual answer when 260P holds the load but the blend stops flowing.

Used on: Feed Additive Flow & Carrier · Agrochemical Carrier

CENSIL 280P

Precipitated silica powder

The high-loading candidate. For choline chloride, organic acids and other demanding liquids where the target loading is the binding constraint and the powder still has to discharge evenly.

Used on: Feed Additive Flow & Carrier · Agrochemical Carrier

Porous silica

Higher pore volume and surface development. These grades carry, stabilise and adsorb rather than simply keeping a powder loose.

CENSIL 310

Porous silica-based material

A moderate-surface porous route for food powders, brewing stabilisation, toothpaste and flavour carriers. The general-purpose choice when structure matters more than sheer capacity.

Used on: Food Powder Flow · Oral Care Silica · Industrial Fluoride Removal

CENSIL 320

Porous silica-based material

The high-surface porous grade. Used in food and oral-care work where adsorption and structure both matter, and it is our preferred base material for fluoride-removal functionalisation screening — as a candidate substrate, not a finished adsorbent.

Used on: Food Powder Flow · Oral Care Silica · Industrial Fluoride Removal

CENSIL 500

High pore-volume silica

Supplied as a coating matting grade, and used on the fluoride track as a high pore-volume comparison substrate when a screening programme needs a second reference point.

Why the numbers are not on this page

Specification tables age badly. Figures drift between supplier revisions, test methods differ between catalogues, and a number read out of context says very little about whether a material will work in your formulation.

So we publish the selection logic and issue the data on request. You receive the current revision, the test method behind each figure, and a test plan written around your actual process.

Samples, minimum order and how a trial runs

What to expect between the first email and the first container.

Samples

Free of charge for qualified enquiries: a main sample plus one or two comparison grades, typically 0.5–2 kg each, with a short test plan. Courier cost is usually carried by the customer for the first shipment.

Minimum order

Typical first order is 200 kg to 1 tonne — enough to run a production trial, not a warehouse commitment. Mixed grades on one order are accepted; we will confirm the exact MOQ per grade with the quotation.

Packing and shipping

Multi-wall paper bags or PE-lined bags on pallets; bulk bags on request. FOB / CIF / DAP as agreed, sea freight from Xiamen or Fuzhou, air or courier for samples and urgent small lots.

Lead time

Samples ship within a few working days. Production orders are confirmed against stock and production schedule at quotation, with the documentation set (TDS, COA, SDS) issued before shipment.

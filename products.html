Why the numbers are not on this page

Specification tables age badly. Figures drift between supplier revisions, test methods differ between catalogues, and a number read out of context says very little about whether a material will work in your formulation.

So we publish the selection logic and issue the data on request. You receive the current revision, the test method behind each figure, and a test plan written around your actual process.

What you receive

01
Technical data sheet, current revision
02
Specification sheet and COA
03
SDS / MSDS and use statement
04
A suggested test plan

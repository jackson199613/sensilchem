We do not publish specification tables. Documents are issued per enquiry, at the current supplier revision, with a test plan written around your process.

From process profile to repeat order

01

Process profiling

We start from the application problem — current material, formulation or resin, equipment, and the target you are trying to hit.

02

Documentation & compliance boundary

We confirm what product documentation, supplier files and importer responsibilities the destination market requires before anything ships.

03

Sample & comparison testing

Main sample plus comparison grades, with a test plan written around your actual problem.

04

Application validation

Bench trial, then scale-up. We read the results with you rather than handing over a datasheet.

05

Small-batch & repeat supply

Small volumes and multiple grades to begin with, moving to a steady reorder rhythm once it works.

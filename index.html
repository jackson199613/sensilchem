Eighteen CENSIL grades, in six families

What each family is for, and which grades sit in it. We describe the selection logic rather than publish specification tables — data sheets, specifications and COA are issued per enquiry so the figures you receive are the current revision, matched to your application.

Carriers & flow aids

CENSIL 260P · 265P · 280P

One absorption gradient, three steps. Take a liquid active onto a dry particle and keep the resulting powder discharging evenly — for premixes, food blends and WP/WG formulations.

Porous silica

CENSIL 310 · 320 · 500

Higher pore volume and surface development, for food powders, brewing and oral care — and as candidate substrates for fluoride-removal functionalisation screening.

High-purity fine powder

CENSIL 150

A fine-powder route for oral-care paste structure and appearance, confirmed on your own paste rather than on a datasheet.

Coating matting silica

CENSIL 230W · 525L · 560N

Specially treated, organically treated and untreated. The surface treatment, not the chemistry, usually decides which one fits your resin system.

Defoamer silica

CENSIL 17 · 698 · 688

A hydrophobic route for oil-based systems, plus two high-absorption grades for turning a liquid defoamer into a powder that still flows.

Film antiblock & masterbatch

CENSIL 773 · 779 · 185P · 165GR · 185GR

Low-iron powders where film optics matter, a conventional comparison route, and granular grades for stable masterbatch feeding.

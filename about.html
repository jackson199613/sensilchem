Who We Are

Longyan Shenghe Trading Co., Ltd is a professional chemicals trading company with over 8 years of experience, specializing in precipitated silica and other chemical products. Under our CENSIL® and Sensilchem® brands, we guarantee efficiency, reliability, and cooperation.

Through these principles and our dedication to honest and win-win trade, high quality service and sustainable development, we have established stable and long-lasting relationships with various renowned enterprises at home and abroad, garnering great support and trust from our valued clients.

Since our establishment, we have dedicated ourselves to the research and development of our products, and our factory holds the following certificates: FAMI-QS, SGS, ISO9001:2008, ISO14001:2004.

Our internal management strictly follows FAMI-QS standards to continuously improve our quality of management, ensuring high-quality product and service delivery.

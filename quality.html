Six controlled production stages

Consistency between lots is a process question, not a certificate question.

01

Reaction & precipitation

Silicate and acid are reacted under controlled temperature, concentration and dosing rate — the stage that sets particle structure.

02

Filtration & washing

Filter pressing and washing remove salts; wash endpoint controls residual sulphate and conductivity.

03

Drying

Spray or flash drying fixes moisture and bulk density, and largely determines how the powder later flows.

04

Classification & packing

Milling and classification set the particle size distribution; packing is sampled per lot for COA.

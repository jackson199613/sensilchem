SILSEP® - Integrated Feed Safety & Animal Health Management

🛡️ Mold Guard

A comprehensive feed defense system that proactively safeguards feed quality and animal health from mold growth and mycotoxin contamination. Our integrated approach combines preventive and curative actions to ensure feed safety and extend shelf life.

  • Adsorbs – Its unique structure provides a vast surface area to strongly bind key mycotoxins (e.g., aflatoxins, DON), preventing intestinal absorption.
  • Eradicates – Natural active ingredients disrupt mold cell membranes, inhibit growth, and provide antioxidant support to preserve feed integrity.

SILSEP Mold Guard delivers complete coverage—physically neutralizing toxins and biologically inhibiting mold. This integrated strategy secures your feed quality from storage to consumption.

💪 Immunity

An advanced dual-pathway health management system designed to preemptively protect your stock. It shifts focus from treating diseases to systematically preventing them by strengthening internal defenses and buffering against external stress.

  • Direct Immune Activation – Natural enhancers trigger immune pathways, rapidly boosting non-specific responses and phagocytic activity of immune cells.
  • Barrier & Microbiome Support – Probiotics colonize the gut, competitively exclude pathogens, and release metabolites that strengthen intestinal integrity.
  • Multi-Target Regulation – Selected plant extracts provide immune-stimulating, antioxidant, antimicrobial, and prebiotic effects through synergistic pathways.

🐟 Intestinal Health

An integrated additive system that holistically manages the internal health chain of aquatic animals—from digestion and gut integrity to liver function.

  • Probiotics – Colonize the gut, inhibit pathogens, and produce metabolites to repair the intestinal barrier.
  • Prebiotics – Selectively nourish beneficial bacteria while enhancing immunity and reducing inflammation.
  • Organic Acids & Salts – Directly energize intestinal cells, strengthen the physical barrier.
  • Functional Amino Acids/Peptides – Provide energy and signaling to alleviate oxidative stress.

🔬 Digestive

A synergistic feed solution that systematically overcomes key bottlenecks in aquatic digestion and absorption.

  • Break Down – Enzymes pre-digest complex proteins, fats, and starches into readily absorbable units.
  • Emulsify – Bile acids prime fats for efficient enzymatic breakdown and absorption.
  • Optimize – Probiotics sustain a healthy gut environment for stable, long-term digestive function.

💧 Integrated Water Management

A synergistic ecosystem strategy that moves beyond treating symptoms to holistically manage water quality. We integrate microbial, chemical, and oxygenation technologies.

  • Microbial Purification – Selected probiotics decompose daily organic waste, convert toxins (ammonia, nitrite), and competitively inhibit pathogens.
  • Digestive Optimization – Intestinal probiotics enhance feed digestion and nutrient absorption.
  • Mineral Remediation – Functional minerals adsorb ammonia, nitrite, heavy metals, and organic particles in water.
  • Bio-transformation – External bio-enzymes convert dissolved ammonia and nitrite into non-toxic nitrate.

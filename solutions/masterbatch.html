Functional Masterbatch Dispersion

For antiblock, functional masterbatch, compounding and film-additive masterbatch plants. Granular and low-iron high-purity silica improve feeding, dispersion, loading and the functional stability of the downstream film.

Quick selection

Granular feeding, base
CENSIL 165GR
Granular, higher surface
CENSIL 185GR
Powder route, appearance-led
CENSIL 779

Where we add value

  • Carry antiblock and anti-adhesion functions stably into PE, PP and PET carrier-resin masterbatch.
  • Granular material improves feeding stability and reduces dust-management difficulty.
  • Judge suitability for production through dispersion, screen-pack pressure and downstream film performance.

Who this track serves

  • Antiblock, anti-adhesion and functional masterbatch manufacturers.
  • Compounding plants, film-additive masterbatch producers and plastic-additive blenders.
  • Masterbatch channels serving film, packaging and engineering-plastics customers.

Grades on this track

Two granular grades aimed at stable feeding and dust control in masterbatch production, plus a low-iron high-purity powder route for customers with tighter downstream appearance requirements.

How to run the trial

Test dimensionWhat to watchWhat it tells you
Feeding and dustFeed stability and dust controlTells you plant operability
Masterbatch dispersionAgglomerates, specks or uneven dispersionTells you downstream appearance
Screen-pack pressureMelt filter pressure riseTells you extrusion stability
LoadingWhether processing holds at the target loadingTells you commercial viability
Downstream filmOpening, haze, clarity, coefficient of frictionTells you end-application value

Where we sit against the reference brands

Reference points include W. R. Grace SYLOBLOC® antiblock series, Evonik SPHERILEX® and SIPERNAT® silica for plastics, and PQ / QEMETICA antiblock and functional filler series.

How we approach it

CENSIL 165GR and 185GR come first for granular feeding and masterbatch dispersion; CENSIL 779 is the supplementary low-iron, high-purity route where downstream appearance requirements are tighter. Confirm resin, equipment, loading and target film properties first.
